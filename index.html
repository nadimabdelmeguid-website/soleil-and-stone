Why Physical Hardware Teams Need Dedicated Local Gathering Spaces

Unlike pure software, physical product creators face supply chain, manufacturing, and prototyping hurdles that require hyper-local cross-pollination. Informal, recurring gatherings build the trust required to share regional supplier networks and technical trade-offs.
