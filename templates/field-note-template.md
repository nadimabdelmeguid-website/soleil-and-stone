Start with a strong observation, tension, or idea.

Avoid spending the first few paragraphs explaining what the article is going to say. Get directly into the insight.

## First Key Idea

Explain the observation.

Use concrete examples where possible.

## Second Key Idea

Develop the argument.

Connect what you observed to a broader lesson.

## What This Changes

Explain why the observation matters.

What should founders, engineers, operators, or ecosystem builders do differently?

## Final Thought

End on one strong idea rather than summarizing the entire article.

A good closing line should be something worth remembering or quoting.
